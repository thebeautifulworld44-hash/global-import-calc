How to Calculate Import Costs

Understand the main costs before placing an international order.

1. Product Cost

Multiply the quantity you are buying by the price per unit.

2. Shipping and Insurance

Add the freight and insurance charges applicable to your shipment.

3. Customs Duty and Tax

Enter the rates you have verified for your product and destination.

4. Total Landed Cost

Add the estimated import costs to understand your overall expense.

5. Cost Per Unit

Divide the estimated total landed cost by the quantity imported.

6. Potential Profit

Compare your expected sales revenue with your estimated landed cost.
